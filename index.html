Expert Tips &
Oral Health Guide

Knowledge is the first step to a healthier smile. Here are essential tips from Dr. Reema Shah on maintaining excellent oral health at home.

Proper Brushing Technique

Most people brush incorrectly. The right technique makes all the difference in preventing cavities and gum disease.

  • Use a soft-bristled toothbrush at a 45-degree angle to gums
  • Brush in gentle circular motions — never scrub side to side
  • Brush for a full 2 minutes, twice daily (morning and night)
  • Don't forget your tongue — it harbors bacteria too
  • Replace your toothbrush every 3 months
Flossing & Rinsing

Brushing alone misses 40% of your tooth surfaces. Flossing and rinsing complete your oral care routine.

  • Floss at least once daily, preferably before bedtime
  • Use a C-shape motion to wrap floss around each tooth
  • Use an antibacterial mouthwash for added protection
  • Rinse with plain water after every meal
  • Avoid mouthwashes with high alcohol content
Diet & Your Teeth

What you eat directly affects your dental health. Small dietary changes can dramatically reduce your risk of cavities.

  • Limit sugary snacks and acidic beverages like sodas
  • Eat calcium-rich foods — dairy, leafy greens, almonds
  • Drink plenty of water, especially fluoridated water
  • Avoid tobacco products — they stain and damage gums
  • Chew sugar-free gum after meals to stimulate saliva
Regular Dental Visits

Prevention is always better — and cheaper — than cure. Regular check-ups catch problems before they become serious.

  • Visit your dentist every 6 months for a routine check-up
  • Get professional scaling done at least twice a year
  • Don't wait for pain — early treatment saves teeth
  • Ask about dental X-rays to detect hidden problems
  • Children should start dental visits from age 1

Is root canal treatment painful?
Absolutely not. With modern anesthesia and our gentle techniques, most patients report feeling completely comfortable throughout the procedure. We prioritize your comfort above all.
What is biomimetic dentistry?
Biomimetic dentistry is an advanced approach that preserves maximum tooth structure. Instead of preparing entire teeth for crowns, we restore only the damaged portion, maintaining your natural tooth strength and longevity.
Do you offer dental implants?
Yes. Dr. Reema is a certified implantologist offering comprehensive dental implant solutions — from single-tooth implants to complex multi-tooth and full-mouth restorations, all with proven success rates.
