Meet Dr. Reema Shah

BDS • Registration No. 41615A

Dr. Reema Shah is a passionate dental professional with over a decade of dedicated practice in advanced restorative and cosmetic dentistry. Her journey in dentistry is driven by a singular belief: every patient deserves a smile they're proud to share.

With specialization in biomimetic restorations, implantology, and smile aesthetics, Dr. Shah combines clinical excellence with genuine compassion. She's earned a reputation for her meticulous attention to detail, innovative treatment approaches, and ability to make even anxious patients feel completely at ease.

What sets Dr. Shah apart is her commitment to tooth preservation. Rather than aggressive interventions, she employs conservative, advanced techniques that maintain maximum natural tooth structure. Her patients consistently praise her calm demeanor, clear communication, and life-changing results.

Beyond clinical excellence, Dr. Shah believes in transparent pricing and honest recommendations — never upselling, always prioritizing what's genuinely best for her patients' oral health and overall wellbeing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your consultation fee?
Our consultation starts at ₹200, which is completely refundable if you proceed with treatment. We believe in transparent, honest pricing with zero hidden charges.
Is root canal treatment painful?
Absolutely not. With modern anesthesia and our gentle techniques, most patients report feeling completely comfortable throughout the root canal treatment procedure. We prioritize your comfort above all.
What is biomimetic dentistry?
Biomimetic dentistry is an advanced approach that preserves maximum tooth structure. Instead of preparing entire teeth for crowns, we restore only the damaged portion, maintaining your natural tooth strength and longevity.
Do you offer dental implants?
Yes. Dr. Reema is a certified implantologist offering comprehensive dental implant solutions — from single-tooth implants to complex multi-tooth and full-mouth restorations, all with proven success rates.
What are your clinic hours?
Monday to Saturday: 10:30 AM - 2:00 PM and 5:30 PM - 9:00 PM. We're also available for emergencies by prior appointment on Sundays. WhatsApp us anytime for scheduling.
